Smart energy monitoring

Understand your energy. Use it smarter.

Every day, electricity is wasted in small ways: lights left on in empty rooms, fans running when nobody is present, and appliances drawing power longer than needed. These habits may seem harmless in isolation, but repeated across homes, classrooms, offices, and workplaces, they add up to unnecessary energy use and higher bills.

Much of the electricity we use is generated by burning fossil fuels. When that happens, energy waste also means avoidable carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions, which contribute to climate change. Saving electricity is therefore not only a way to reduce costs—it is a practical step toward using resources responsibly, reducing demand, and protecting the environment.

Electricity is essential for lighting, comfort, education, healthcare, communication, and daily life. The goal is not to use less than we need; it is to avoid using energy when it serves no purpose. That starts with awareness: knowing how much power is being used, which rooms are consuming it, and whether connected devices are still needed.

That is why we created PowerSave 360. It brings room-wise electricity measurements, energy statistics, occupancy awareness, and supported light and fan controls into one interface. By making usage easier to see and understand, PowerSave 360 helps users notice waste, make informed decisions, and build more responsible everyday habits.

Review available energy data, explore trends, and download reports from the application to keep records and support your energy-saving decisions. Actual readings and report contents depend on the data collected by your connected system.

Product overview

Designed to make monitoring easier

PowerSave 360 is an energy-monitoring project built around connected sensors, a controller and a web dashboard. Its aim is to make electrical readings and room status easier to view and understand.

  • ✓Bring key electrical readings into one dashboard.
  • ✓Review information for individual monitored rooms.
