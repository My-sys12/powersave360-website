Product overview

Designed to make monitoring easier

PowerSave 360 is an energy-monitoring project built around connected sensors, a controller and a web dashboard. Its aim is to make electrical readings and room status easier to view and understand.

  • ✓Bring key electrical readings into one dashboard.
  • ✓Review information for individual monitored rooms.
  • ✓Access supported light and fan controls from the dashboard.
  • ✓Use occupancy information as an input for more informed energy decisions.

About us

About PowerSave 360

PowerSave 360 is a connected energy-monitoring project focused on presenting useful electrical and room-status information in a clear, accessible interface. The system's live features depend on the connected hardware, network and dashboard configuration.
